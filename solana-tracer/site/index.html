KOL Wallet Networks

Wallets that moved $500 or more in SOL, USDC or USDT directly to or from a KOL's public wallet. Open a KOL to see the map and the ledger; open a private wallet to see who it moved money with.

Only plain transfers count. Any transaction that touches a DEX, AMM, bonding curve, liquidity pool or other program is excluded, and pool or vault accounts never appear.
"Private wallet" means an ordinary wallet with fewer than 1,000 transactions. It is a suspected side wallet, not a confirmed one: friends, OTC deals, payments and team wallets look the same on chain.
"Exchange / service" marks busy addresses (1,000+ transactions) such as exchange hot wallets and bots. They are shown but not expanded.
